Plan the Week Ahead

A week of meals is far easier to manage when it is written down before you shop. Choose three evening meals that share ingredients, cook a double portion of one of them, and carry the second portion into the next day as lunch.

Leave one evening open for whatever is still in the fridge. That single habit removes most of the waste at the end of the week, and it removes the decision you are least able to make on a Thursday evening.

Write the list before you go, and then shop from the list rather than from the shelves.

One Shopping Trip

Group the list by where things sit in the shop: Dry goods, fresh produce, and the cold section. One trip through the shop is quicker, and it keeps you out of the aisles you did not plan to visit.

Buy the fresh items last, so that they spend the least time out of the fridge on the way home.

Cook Once, Eat Twice

A pot of soup, a tray of roasted vegetables, or a pan of pasta sauce will all keep for two or three days in the fridge. Cook the batch on the evening you have the most time, and portion it before you sit down to eat.

Label each container with the date, so that nothing is kept for longer than it should be.

This Week at a Glance

One evening meal carries into the next day as lunch, and one pot covers two evenings. The weekend is left open.

Equipment

Plan around the equipment you can actually use.

  • Kettle
  • Microwave
  • One hotplate
  • One sharp knife
  • Two sealed containers
