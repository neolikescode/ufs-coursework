Kitchen Basics

A small kitchen works best when it is kept simple. Store dry goods in sealed containers, wash up as you go, and keep one sharp knife rather than a drawer full of blunt ones.

Shared kitchens need a shared routine. Agree on who cleans what, label anything in the fridge that belongs to you, and never leave a pot soaking overnight.

Plan Ahead

The simplest habit to build is what students here call cook once, eat twice, which means making enough of an evening meal to carry into the next day as lunch.

Write your list before you go shopping, and then shop from the list rather than from the shelves.
