The Idea

CampusBite started as a shared document between four students in the same residence, all of whom were cooking for themselves for the first time. The document grew, other residences asked for a copy, and it became simpler to put everything on a web page than to keep sending the file around.

Every idea on this site has been cooked, eaten, and repeated by a student at least once.

Share a Meal

Anyone who studies here may contribute. A useful entry names the equipment it needs, lists the ingredients in the order in which they are used, and keeps the method to five steps or fewer.

Ideas that need an oven, a food processor, or an hour of attention are welcome, but they are marked clearly, because most readers have none of those three.
