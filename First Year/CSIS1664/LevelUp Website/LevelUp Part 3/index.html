Featured Tip of the Week

The Pomodoro technique breaks study time into focused 25 minute blocks, each followed by a short five minute break. After four blocks, take a longer break of 20 to 30 minutes. Students find it easiest to start with a single block a day and build up from there.

  • Switch off every notification before the block starts.
  • Write down the one task you will finish in this block.
  • Leave your desk during the break, even if only for a minute.
