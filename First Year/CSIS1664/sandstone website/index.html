Sandstone Singers

Four-part harmony in the eastern Free State

About the choir

The Sandstone Singers meet every Tuesday evening in the old town hall on Market Street, where the thick sandstone walls give the room a warmth that singers notice from the very first note. Anyone who enjoys singing is welcome and there is no audition. Dates for the coming season are published by the Free State Choral Festival.

A reviewer who heard us last winter called the choir the finest small choir in the province, and the members have not stopped repeating the phrase since. The review appeared in The Eastern Free State Courier.

Voice parts

We sing in four parts. New members are placed after one rehearsal, and nobody is asked to sing alone.

  • Upper voices
    1. Soprano
    2. Alto
  • Lower voices
    1. Tenor
    2. Bass
